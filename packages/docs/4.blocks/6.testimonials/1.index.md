Five independent blocks. Install the layout you need and replace its demonstration content.

- [Centered](/blocks/testimonials/testimonial-centered): A centered quote with a company mark and author portrait.
- [Split](/blocks/testimonials/testimonial-split): A masked portrait beside a compact quote with fading dividers.
- [Staggered](/blocks/testimonials/testimonial-staggered): Staggered quote cards with extended borders and corner accents.
- [Split Grid](/blocks/testimonials/testimonial-split-grid): An asymmetric quote grid with full-width dividers.
- [Wall](/blocks/testimonials/testimonial-wall): A responsive testimonial wall with avatars and subtle grid patterns.
