# Removing the Efferd marketing blocks

> Thirty-three marketing blocks ported from Svelte Efferd are leaving the registry. Pricing, workspace screens, and the other components stay.

PropShow Kit is removing the marketing blocks that were ported from [SikandarJODD/sv-efferd](https://github.com/SikandarJODD/sv-efferd) and, through that port, from upstream Efferd. The registry no longer publishes them, and the docs pages for those groups are gone.

## Why they are leaving

The Vue sources can still be traced to the MIT-licensed Svelte repository, Copyright (c) 2026 SikandarJODD. That repository credits the original blocks to Shaban. On the product side, we are tightening risk exposure anyway.

The current terms at [efferd.com/license](https://efferd.com/license) (page last updated October 31, 2026) are a different grant from that MIT text. They allow commercial use inside a product someone ships, and they prohibit passing the blocks on as blocks: selling, sharing, or publishing them on their own or as a UI kit, component library, template, theme, starter, or registry. PropShow Kit is a registry. Keeping those blocks here would carry the risk that redistribution through this registry does not match the current website terms. We are removing the blocks to avoid that risk.

## What was removed

Thirty-three blocks, in seven groups:

- **Call to Action** — five blocks: split, centered, corner accent, trial card, and newsletter.
- **Testimonials** — five blocks: centered, split, staggered, split grid, and wall.
- **Integrations** — five blocks: strip, grid, split, floating, and cluster.
- **Logo Cloud** — five blocks: grid, decorated, marquee, blur, and wrap.
- **Header** — five blocks: simple, framed, mega, floating, and pill, including the shared navigation and scroll helpers.
- **Hero** — three blocks: spotlight, framed, and product.
- **Features** — five blocks: strip, framed, rails, cards, and grid.

Sample assets that those blocks loaded from `storage.efferd.com` go with them. The registry JSON for these names is no longer published.

## What stays

These were not part of that port, and they remain:

- **Nuxt UI pricing** — [Pricing Plan](/components/pricing-plan), [Pricing Plans](/components/pricing-plans), and [Pricing Table](/components/pricing-table).
- **Workspace blocks** — [project overview](/blocks/project-overview), [team members](/blocks/team-members), and [workspace settings](/blocks/workspace-settings).
- **Other components** adapted from Kumo, ReUI, Medusa, and original kit work: Frame, Layer Card, Layer Dialog, Inline Tip, Status Badge, Copy, Inline Copy Text, Filters, Slider, and Delete Resource.

The [blocks index](/blocks) now lists the three workspace screens. The [component index](/components) is unchanged.

The dated list is [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/prop-show/prop-show-kit/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md) and [What changed](/guide/changelog). A tour of what the site ships after this removal is [What PropShow Kit ships today](/blog/what-ships-today).
